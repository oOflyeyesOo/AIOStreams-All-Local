Public & Community Databases — optional

Credential Sharing Notice: Torrentio operates as an external hosted index. When enabled in Debrid mode, your provider API key is passed to Torrentio so it can resolve and unrestrict cached cloud streams. If you prefer your debrid keys never leave your server, uncheck Torrentio to route search queries exclusively through your local scrapers (MediaFusion, Jackett, Comet, TorBox/Real-Debrid direct).

Calls out to the public Torrentio index database (https://torrentio.strem.fun) over the internet to access millions of pre-indexed torrent releases in ~30ms without requiring a multi-year local crawler. Privacy protection: All video streams are proxied through this server's IP address so your client IP is shielded and multiple devices won't trigger debrid provider bans.

Calls out to community Debrid Media Manager (DMM) hashlists over the internet to periodically sync curated torrent hashes into your local Zilean container. This gives you instant (<50ms) local cache hits for popular releases without scraping public trackers from scratch.

Calls out to the public RemuxDB database over the internet to source accurate container probe data (TrueHD 7.1 Atmos, DTS-HD MA, and Dolby Vision / HDR10+ profiles), enabling high-precision ranking and filtering for 4K Remux releases.
